The idea

Make technical checks easier to understand.

DLeo aims to turn selected checks into readable findings, so a developer can decide what to review next instead of relying on a vague “safe” or “unsafe” label.

01 · Overview

What is DLeo?

DLeo is an evolving command-line and diagnostic project. Its current direction focuses on basic website inspection and project-readiness checks. It is being developed incrementally, with accuracy and clear limits taking priority over big claims.

What it is for

Helping a developer spot selected website configuration signals, read a result, and decide what deserves a closer look.

Who it helps

People learning about website reliability and developers who want a lightweight first look before deeper manual review.

03 · Workflow

How to think about a check

A useful result starts a review; it doesn't replace one. The intended workflow stays simple and human-controlled.

Choose a site you are allowed to check

Only inspect websites you own or have permission to assess, and follow the site's rules.

Read the findings carefully

Review the specific signal reported and remember that a missing header or a passing check is only one part of the picture.

Verify before changing anything

Confirm findings independently and test changes safely before using them in production.

04 · Trust and limitations

What DLeo does—and does not—mean

Clear boundaries are part of building a tool people can trust.

Useful for

  • A preliminary look at selected website headers.
  • Surfacing selected configuration or documentation items.
  • Organizing findings for a human to review.

Not a substitute for

  • A complete security audit or penetration test.
  • Antivirus, a firewall, a VPN, or Android device protection.
  • Professional review or independent verification of important findings.

Important safety note

DLeo does not automatically protect your phone or guarantee that a website is safe. These checks have limits, and a “pass” is not proof that no vulnerabilities exist. Don't install software or grant device permissions just because a project page recommends a check.
