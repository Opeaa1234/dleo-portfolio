What is DLeo?

DLeo is being developed to help people inspect selected website signals and understand potential follow-up work. The goal is to make checks easier to read and improvements easier to approach one step at a time.

Project capabilities and direction

🔎

Website checks

Checks selected HTTP security headers and reports findings for further review. It does not test every possible vulnerability.

🩺

Readiness diagnostics

Doctor-style checks can highlight selected configuration and documentation items that may need attention.

📋

Readable reports

Reporting is intended to make results easier to inspect and support careful follow-up rather than automatic changes.

What DLeo does not claim

DLeo is not a complete security audit, antivirus product, firewall, VPN, or automatic protection for your Android phone. A website check cannot guarantee that a site or device is safe. Treat results as one input and review important decisions carefully.

What comes next?

Future work may include better reporting, more tested checks, and a friendlier interface. Features will be described as available only after they have been implemented and tested. This public page is an overview; private development resources are not linked here, and no account is needed to read it.
